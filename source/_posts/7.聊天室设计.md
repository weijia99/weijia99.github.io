## 问题
### 如何查询threadid
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/weijia99/sync2hexo/master/f11457aff228ba7d6f709a2a4fab1496.png?token=AGECE2J76KVMI6DMQQZ6FBDG72W4W)

```plain
SELECT thread_id FROM threads WHERE user_ids = SORT(ARRAY[user_id_A, user_id_B]) AND thread_type = 'private'

```

直接进行排序后，然后查询uid就可以得到，使用haxi加快
